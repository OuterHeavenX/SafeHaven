# Game Design — Stage 1: The Fallen Valley

The Order of the Haven enters a corrupted valley under a crimson moon. A Haven Construction Wagon is the foothold. Deploying it creates the Haven Keep and unlocks the base loop: power, Gold hauling, infantry, Essence, advanced technology, heavy war machines, expansion, then an assault on the Crimson Covenant fortress.

Resources: Gold and Essence are currencies; Power is generated capacity. Gatherers physically travel, gather, return, and unload before currency is credited. Low power disables powered production.

Combat uses BLADE, PROJECTILE, SIEGE, HOLY and ARCANE damage against LIGHT, ARMORED, STRUCTURE and SUPERNATURAL armor. Units earn veterancy. Projectile weapons create simulated projectiles rather than instant hits.

Victory: destroy the Covenant Fortress. Defeat: lose the Haven Keep after deployment.
